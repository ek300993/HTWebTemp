# Looking after your site

Everything on this site is editable from the WordPress dashboard. You don't need
to touch any code, and you can't break the design by editing text or swapping
photos — the colours and fonts are locked to your brand, so whatever you type
comes out looking right.

---

## First five things

1. **Check your logo.** Appearance → Editor → click the header → click the logo
   to replace it. It appears in the header and the footer.
2. **Tell the inquiry form where to send.** Settings → General → **Gift basket
   inquiries** — put the email address you actually read in there. Then send
   yourself a test message from the Contact page to make sure it arrives (check
   your spam folder too; see "Your inquiry form" below).
3. **Add your photos.** Every grey box with a little picture icon is an empty
   image — click it, upload, done. There are a lot of them waiting: four occasion
   tiles on the home page, most of the rows on Browse Baskets, two photos of you
   on the About page, and both galleries.
4. **Add your first basket.** Baskets → Add New (see below).
5. **Check your details.** Settings → General for the site name; Pages → Contact
   for your turnaround times and anything else customers should know.

## Adding a basket

**Baskets → Add New.**

- **Title** — the basket's name, e.g. "Housewarming Basket".
- **Basket Photo** (right-hand sidebar) — the main picture. Always set one; a
  basket with no photo leaves a blank space in the grids on your homepage.
- **Occasions** (right-hand sidebar) — tick one or more. These are the eight
  kinds of basket: New Baby, New Home, Graduation, Weddings, Retirement,
  Birthday, For Teachers, Thinking of You.
- **Excerpt** (right-hand sidebar) — one short line, e.g. "For a brand new front
  door". This shows under the photo in grids, so keep it to a few words.
- **The main area** — already set up with what each basket needs: a short
  description and a **What Went Inside** list. Just fill them in.

Hit Publish and it appears automatically on the shop archive, in its occasion,
and in the "Recently Made" row on your Gallery page. **You never have to edit a
page to add a basket.**

**These are examples, not a fixed menu.** The whole site is written on the basis
that every basket is custom — the shop page says so, and each basket page says
it's an example rather than something to order exactly. So don't worry about
listing a basket you can't repeat item for item; that's the point of showing it.

**There are no prices on the site**, for the same reason — you quote each
inquiry yourself. If you ever want a price on a basket page, type it into the
basket like any other text.

To add a new occasion: Baskets → Occasions.

## Editing a page

**Pages → click the page → click the text → type.** Save with Update.

Each page is built from sections. Some sections are locked so you can change
every word and picture but can't accidentally pull the layout apart. Others —
the occasion grid, the reviews, the basket row — are fully open so you can add to
them.

**To add a section:** click the **+** button, scroll to the **Happy Turtle**
group, and pick one. They're all pre-styled: hero, four promises, occasion grid,
the Browse Baskets rows, featured baskets, both About sections, the gallery
sections, how it works, reviews, and the green call-to-action band.

**To reuse a section's look:** click the section, three-dot menu → Duplicate.
Then change the words. That's how you add a ninth kind of basket to Browse
Baskets, or another occasion tile to the home page.

**If you add a kind of basket to Browse Baskets,** duplicate a whole band (the
coloured strip, not just the text), swap its background so it still alternates
cream/sand with its neighbours, and add a matching pill to the row of jump links
at the top of the page.

## Your pages

| Page | What's on it |
|---|---|
| **Home** | The big picture, four promises, the occasion tiles, your introduction |
| **About** | Nice to Meet You, and Personal Life — both want a photo of you |
| **Browse Baskets** | One row per kind of basket, with a description and an example photo |
| **How It Works** | The four steps, from first form to handing it over |
| **Gallery** | Recently Made (fills itself), then two photo galleries |
| **Reviews** | Six of your Google reviews |
| **Contact** | Your email and phone, and the inquiry form |

**The menu is just your pages, in order.** Add a page and it appears in the menu.
To move one, Pages → hover it → Quick Edit → **Order** (lower numbers come
first). Nothing else to keep in sync.

## The two galleries

The Gallery page has two photo grids — one for baskets, one for individual items
like mugs, onesies and sweatshirts. They start with a few slots each and are
meant to grow.

**To add photos:** click the gallery, then the **+** at the end of it, and upload
as many as you like at once. The grid rearranges itself — you can't leave a
half-empty row. Fifteen or twenty in each is a good target; more is fine.

**To remove a grey placeholder:** click it and press Delete.

## Your inquiry form

The Contact page has a working form: email, phone, what they're after, and a
message. When somebody sends one it arrives in your inbox, and hitting Reply
goes straight back to them.

**Where it sends:** Settings → General → **Gift basket inquiries**.

**Test it before you tell anyone about the site.** Fill it in yourself and check
the message actually arrives. Website email is the one thing that quietly fails
on some hosting — if it doesn't turn up, or it lands in spam, ask whoever set the
site up to install an SMTP plugin. It's a ten-minute job and it's worth doing at
launch rather than after a customer's inquiry goes missing.

**What it doesn't do:** photo uploads. If you want customers sending reference
pictures through the form, that needs a form plugin — a small job for a
developer, and the form on the page would be swapped for theirs.

The dropdown of what they're after is built from your Occasions list, so adding
an occasion adds it to the form too.

## Small styling touches

Select some text and the right-hand sidebar has a **Styles** tab with a few
options built for this site:

- **Heart Divider** — the little `— ♥ —` rule used under headings.
- **Handwritten** — the script font, for lines like *thank you for supporting my
  small business!*
- **Eyebrow Label** — the small spaced-out capitals above a heading.

---

## Things worth knowing

**You can always undo.** Every page keeps a full history — open the page, click
Revisions in the right sidebar, and roll back to any earlier version.

**Colours are intentionally limited.** The picker only offers your greens and
creams. That's on purpose — it's what stops the site drifting off-brand over
time. If you genuinely need a new colour, that's a small change for a developer.

**Preview before you publish.** Top right → Preview → in a new tab. Worth doing
on your phone, since the layouts rearrange on small screens.

**The big picture at the top of the home page is a special one.** It's a wide
version of the photo with the left-hand side fading into a soft background, so
the words have somewhere clean to sit. That fade is baked into the picture
itself, which means uploading an ordinary photo in its place won't look right —
the words would land on top of the basket. If you ever want a different picture
up there, that's a small job for a developer rather than a swap in the editor —
there's a second, plain copy of it behind the scenes for phones and tablets,
where the words sit straight on the photo. Everything else on the site is still a
click-and-upload.

**Your Instagram is linked** in the footer and on the Contact page. To change the
address: Appearance → Editor → click the footer → click the Instagram icon.

**Keep WordPress and your plugins updated.** Dashboard → Updates. This is the
main thing that keeps the site secure. Five minutes a month.
